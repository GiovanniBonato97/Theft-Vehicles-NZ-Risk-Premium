# Theft Vehicles in New Zealand (Risk Premia Analysis)

A Power BI dashboard that helps insurance companies set premiums by showing where, when and which vehicles are most likely to be stolen in New Zealand (2021–2022).

📄 [View the full report (PDF)](<Theft Vehicles in New Zealand (Risk Premia analysis).pdf>)

## Key findings
- **Thefts rose 73%**, from 1,649 in 2021 to 2,860 in 2022.
- **Most thefts happened between October 2021 and March 2022**, peaking at 787 in March 2022, before dropping back below 100 a month.
- **Auckland is the highest-risk region**, with 1,620 thefts (36% of the total), followed by Bay of Plenty, Waikato and Wellington. Southland, Taranaki, Hawke's Bay and Nelson had the fewest.
- **96% of stolen vehicles were standard (non-luxury) makes.** Utes like the Toyota Hilux and Ford Courier were the most stolen models, and silver was the most common colour (34%).
- **The forecast shows thefts staying close to recent levels**, with a slight upward trend.

## Recommendation
Premiums should be based on region and vehicle model rather than vehicle value: higher for Auckland and for popular utes, since luxury cars are rarely targeted.

**Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX
